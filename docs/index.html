FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDF Master really 100% free? ▼
Yes. PDF Master is an open-source project licensed under Apache 2.0. There are no premium tiers, no hidden subscriptions, and no advertisements.
Can I use PDF Master to open PDFs from WhatsApp or Chrome? ▼
Yes! PDF Master registers as a system PDF handler in AndroidManifest.xml. When you tap any PDF link or attachment in another app, PDF Master appears in the app chooser.
Where are processed PDFs saved? ▼
All generated PDFs are automatically saved directly to your phone's Downloads/PDFMaster/ folder, where you can access them via any file manager or share them via WhatsApp, Drive, and Email.
